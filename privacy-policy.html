This Privacy Policy explains how RavenByte Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you play Stack Rush: Color Tower. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This app is designed to be simple, fun, and respectful of your data.

Information We Do Not Collect

Stack Rush: Color Tower does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your device's contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Any financial or payment information
  • Your browsing history or data from other apps

Local Game Data

The game saves certain data locally on your device only to provide a better experience. This includes:

  • Your high score and best performance records
  • Number of games played and perfect stacks
  • Coins collected and shop preferences
  • Selected game themes and settings

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

Stack Rush: Color Tower does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not use:

  • Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics
  • Facebook SDK or any social login
  • AdMob or any advertising networks
  • Crash reporting tools that collect user data

Internet Permission

The app may request internet access solely to load game assets when needed. No personal data is transmitted over the internet at any time.

Children's Privacy

Stack Rush: Color Tower is safe for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since we collect no personal data from anyone, this app fully complies with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar regulations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
